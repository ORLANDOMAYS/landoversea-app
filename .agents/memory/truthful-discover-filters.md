---
name: Truthful Discover filters
description: Product rules for persisted preferences, unrestricted defaults, and unavailable distance filtering.
---

Discover preferences have one server-persisted canonical model, including relationship goal, shared interests, verified-only, long-distance openness, and relocation openness. Clear means ages 18–99, global mode enabled, no list/gender/goal restrictions, and every advanced boolean disabled.

**Why:** Split persistence caused the filter page and card deck to disagree. The product also has no coordinate model or reliable distance values, so an enabled radius control would falsely claim behavior the system cannot perform.

**How to apply:** Hydrate, apply, retry, and clear the complete model through the API. Remove legacy local-only copies. Keep distance controls explicitly unavailable and explanatory until precise-location storage and real distance computation exist.
